Slide 7 — Risk and return I — what beta says, and what it does not

Speaker: Qi Peiyuan | Time: 8:00–9:45 | Duration: 105 seconds | English: about 183 words

English script

Now, let's look at risk. The key point on this slide is that beta measures market risk, and only market risk. Beta is the slope of a stock's excess returns against the market's excess returns. In simple terms, beta tells us how strongly Tesla moves when the market moves. If beta is above one, the stock has tended to move more than the market. If beta is below one, it has tended to move less. But we should remember that beta is only an estimate. It is not a perfect or fixed number. So, we should use beta as a guide to market sensitivity. There is also another type of risk: firm-specific risk. For Tesla, this includes execution, competition, governance, and headlines. The important difference is that firm-specific risk can be reduced by diversification. We can do this by holding a spread of different stocks instead of only one. So, Tesla may be a volatile stock, but that does not mean we should completely avoid it. The key is to hold it as one part of a diversified portfolio, not our whole savings.

中文翻译

接下来我们看看风险。这一页的核心要点是：贝塔衡量的是市场风险，而且只衡量市场风险。贝塔是一只股票的超额收益对市场超额收益回归的斜率。简单来说，贝塔告诉我们大盘波动的时候，Tesla 通常会跟着波动多剧烈。如果贝塔大于 1，说明这只股票的波动幅度往往比大盘更大；如果小于 1，说明波动幅度比大盘小。但我们要记住，贝塔只是一个估算值，它不是一个完美或固定不变的数字，所以只能把它当作衡量市场敏感度的参考。除此之外还有另一类风险：公司特有风险。对 Tesla 来说，这包括执行力、市场竞争、公司治理以及各种新闻舆论。关键的区别在于，公司特有风险是可以通过分散投资来降低的——只要我们同时持有一篮子不同的股票，而不是只押注一只就行。所以，Tesla 确实可能是一只波动很大的股票，但这并不意味着我们要完全避开它。关键在于把它当成多元化投资组合里的一部分，而不是把全部身家都押上去。

Slide 8 — Risk and return II — from CAPM to a hurdle rate

Speaker: Qi Peiyuan | Time: 9:45–11:30 | Duration: 105 seconds | English: about 205 words

English script

Now, let's move from beta to CAPM and the hurdle rate. The main idea is simple: risk must be paid for. The CAPM formula on the slide is: E(R) = Rf + beta × [E(Rm) − Rf]. Here, Rf is the risk-free rate. It is the return we can get for taking no market risk. Beta measures market sensitivity. When beta is higher, the market risk premium is also higher. So, if an investment has more market risk, investors should require a higher return. This leads us to the idea of a hurdle rate. The cost of capital combines the cost of equity with the after-tax cost of debt. This cost of capital becomes the hurdle rate for a project. A project creates value only when its expected return is above this hurdle. So, a higher-risk project needs to generate a higher return. This is important for Tesla because it is a volatile growth company. Tesla faces a high hurdle, so its growth plans need to earn enough return to pay for the risk. This is the discipline behind our recommendation: higher risk is not a reason to reject a stock, as long as the expected return is high enough to compensate for that risk.

中文翻译

现在，我们从贝塔进一步讲到 CAPM 和门槛收益率。核心思路很简单：承担风险就必须有回报来补偿。幻灯片上的 CAPM 公式是：E(R) = Rf + 贝塔 × [E(Rm) − Rf]。这里的 Rf 是无风险利率，也就是不承担市场风险就能拿到的回报。贝塔衡量市场敏感度，贝塔越高，市场风险溢价也就越高。所以，如果一项投资承担的市场风险更大，投资者就应该要求更高的回报。这就引出了门槛收益率的概念。加权平均资本成本把股权成本和税后债务成本结合在了一起，而这个加权平均资本成本就成了一个项目的门槛收益率。只有当项目的预期回报高于这道门槛收益率时，它才算创造价值。因此，风险越高的项目，就越需要产生更高的回报。这一点对 Tesla 尤其重要，因为它是一家高波动的成长型公司。Tesla 面临很高的门槛收益率，所以它的各项扩张计划必须赚到足够的回报，才能抵消对应的风险。这正是我们投资建议背后的纪律：高风险本身不是拒绝一只股票的理由，只要它的预期回报高到足以补偿这份风险就行。

Slide 9 — Options — a right, not an obligation

Speaker: Hu Kai | Time: 11:30–13:30 | Duration: 120 seconds | English: about 187 words

English script

As we just showed, Tesla faces a high hurdle rate. Its growth plans have to create serious value to pay for that risk. That leads to our next question: how do we value future projects when they are still full of uncertainty? If a company had to invest all its money on day one and could never change its mind, a high hurdle rate would kill almost every new idea. Fortunately, business decisions do not work like that. And in Topic 3, we learned the tool for this: options. Think about how a financial option works. When you hold an option, you have the right to act later, but you do not have to. If things go well, you step in and capture the upside. If things go badly, you simply walk away, and your loss stops at your initial cost. Because the downside is capped while the upside stays open, uncertainty takes on a new role. Big swings in possible outcomes actually make the option more valuable, because the good outcomes can be huge. Yuhang will now show how this logic applies to real investment decisions.

中文翻译

正如我们刚才讲的，Tesla 面临很高的门槛收益率，它的增长计划必须创造出足够大的价值，才能覆盖这份风险。这就引出了我们的下一个问题：当未来的新项目还充满不确定性时，我们该怎么评估它们的价值？如果一家公司必须在第一天就把所有钱一次性砸进去、而且再也不能反悔，那这么高的门槛收益率几乎会否掉所有新想法。好在真实的商业决策并不是这样运作的。在 Topic 3 里，我们正好学了解决这个问题的工具：期权。大家可以回想一下金融期权是怎么运作的。当你持有期权时，你有权利在以后采取行动，但并没有义务非做不可。如果进展顺利，你就跟进投入、拿走上行收益；如果情况不好，你直接放弃就行，损失也就停在一开始的投入上。正因为下行风险被封住、而上行空间敞开，不确定性就有了全新的作用：结果波动越大，期权反而越值钱，因为一旦成了，回报会非常可观。接下来，Yuhang 会讲讲这套逻辑是怎么用到真实投资决策里的。

Slide 10 — Real options framework — expand, delay, abandon

Speaker: Fan Yuhang | Time: 13:30–15:00 | Duration: 90 seconds | English: about 199 words

English script

Think of a real option as the same idea, applied to a project. Management can change course as new information comes in. That flexibility is upside an investor pays for. We covered three types. An expansion option means a project with a negative net present value today can still be worth doing. Why? Because it buys you the right to expand later. A delay option means an exclusive right, like a patent, has value even when investing now doesn't pay. And it rises with volatility. An abandonment option means the right to stop. When cash flows disappoint, you cap the downside. The project is worth more. But options aren't magic, and Campusteria proves it. The pilot restaurant had a net present value of negative 21,916 dollars and 84 cents. Its expansion option was worth only 718 dollars and 3 cents. So the answer was still no. Now compare the drug patent. Investing now gives a negative net present value of about 150 million dollars. Yet the seventeen year patent, as a delay option, is still worth 37.12 million dollars. The framework is simple. Find the option. Price it. Then decide. Gao Tian will now run that sequence on Tesla.

中文翻译

实物期权你可以理解成同一套思路，用在具体项目上。新信息一来，管理层就能调整方向。这份灵活性就是投资者愿意付钱的上行空间。我们讲了三种。扩张期权是说，一个项目今天净现值是负的，也照样可能值得做。为什么？因为它买到了以后扩张的权利。延迟期权是说，像专利这样的排他性权利，就算现在投不划算也有价值。而且波动率越高越值钱。放弃期权是说，你有权停手。现金流不如预期时，你就封住了下行风险。项目反而更值钱。但期权不是魔法，Campusteria 就证明了这一点。那家试点餐厅的净现值是负 21,916.84 美元。它的扩张期权只值 718.03 美元。所以结论还是不做。再对比一下医药专利。现在就投，净现值大约是负 1.5 亿美元。但那项 17 年的专利作为延迟期权，依然值 3,712 万美元。框架很简单。先找出期权。给它定价。然后做决定。接下来 Gao Tian 会把这个顺序用到 Tesla 上。

Slide 11 — Tesla real options — Cybertruck out, Cybercab in

Speaker: Gao Tian | Time: 15:00–17:00 | Duration: 120 seconds | English: about 236 words

English script

Now let’s apply the framework to a recent business decision that supports our investment view. Tesla is shutting down Cybertruck production and expanding Cybercab capacity. Cybertruck sales in the United States fell from about 39,000 units in 2024 to about 20,000 in 2025, far below the earlier expectation of 250,000 units a year. In November, Musk said remaining unfilled orders would be completed first, then the production line taken apart. We can see this as the company exercising an abandonment option. The important point, stressed by the course case, is that sunk costs should not decide this. Money already spent on the Cybertruck line is gone either way. The correct comparison is the incremental value of continuing Cybertruck production against converting capacity to Cybercab. A management team willing to drop a flagship product rather than defend sunk costs is protecting capital, which reassure the investors. On the other side, Cybercab, Self-Driving, Optimus and Dojo are expansion and delay options. The Black-Scholes mapping is direct: S is the present value of the cash flows, E is the conversion or investment cost, t is the time before a decision is due, and sigma is the volatility of those cash flows. High uncertainty is exactly what gives these projects big option value. If you wait until the outcome is certain, most of that upside will already be priced in. Next, Qi Peiyuan will show what that means for valuation.

中文翻译

现在我们把这套框架用到一个正在发生、并且能支撑投资逻辑的真实决策上。Tesla 正在逐步关停 Cybertruck 的生产，同时把产能扩向 Cybercab，背后的数字很说明问题。Cybertruck 在美国的销量从 2024 年的大约 39,000 辆掉到了 2025 年的大约 20,000 辆，远低于早先每年 250,000 辆的预期。11 月的时候 Musk 表示，会先把剩下的未交付订单做完，然后拆掉这条产线。我们可以把这看作一次正在行权的放弃期权。正如课程案例反复强调的，沉没成本不应该决定这个选择——以前砸在 Cybertruck 产线上的钱，不管怎样都已经花出去了。真正该对比的，是继续生产 Cybertruck 的增量价值，和扣除改造费用与风险后把产能转给 Cybercab 的价值。一个愿意果断砍掉旗舰产品、而不是死守沉没成本的管理层，是在真正保护资本，这应该能让投资者更放心。另一边，Cybercab、Full Self-Driving、Optimus 和 Dojo 则属于扩张期权和延迟期权。它们跟 Black-Scholes 模型的对应关系非常直接：S 是未来现金流的现值，E 是产线改造或投入成本，t 是必须做出决策前的剩余时间，sigma 则是这些现金流的波动率。高不确定性恰恰是这些项目能带有巨大期权价值的原因。投资者现在买进股票，就相当于把这些期权握在手里，而不是等到结果完全明朗、价值已经被市场涨完之后再进场。接下来，Qi Peiyuan 会讲讲这对估值意味着什么。

Slide 12 — Valuation direction — the price is an options question

Speaker: Qi Peiyuan | Time: 17:00–18:30 | Duration: 90 seconds | English: about 209 words

English script

Finally, let's look at our valuation direction. The key question is: Why is Tesla's price so high? If we value Tesla only by selling cars, the price is difficult to explain. But we think this is the wrong test. The slide shows two parts of Tesla's value. First, we have the car business today. Tesla is already selling cars, and it also has energy and services that are operating and generating cash. Second, we have options that are not yet at scale. These include Robotaxi and FSD, Optimus, energy, and software. The market price is not only paying for the businesses Tesla has today. It is also paying for the possibility that these future businesses become much bigger. Of course, these businesses are still uncertain. But this is where the options idea becomes important. An option gives us a right, not an obligation. If these businesses succeed, there can be significant upside. If they do not, investors can choose to exit. So, uncertainty itself can add value. This is why being early can beat waiting for proof. Our point is not that all these future businesses will definitely succeed. Our point is that the Tesla price reflects both its current business and the potential value of these future options.

中文翻译

最后，我们来看看估值方向。核心问题是：Tesla 的股价为什么这么高？如果只按卖车来给 Tesla 估值，这个价格确实很难解释。但我们认为这种检验标准用错了。幻灯片把 Tesla 的价值拆成了两部分。第一部分是现在的汽车主业：Tesla 已经在卖车了，而且还有正在运营并产生现金流的能源和服务业务。第二部分是还没有放量的期权，包括 Robotaxi 和 FSD、Optimus、能源以及软件。市场给的这个价格，不只是在为 Tesla 今天已经有的业务买单，更是在为这些未来业务做大做强的可能性买单。当然，这些业务目前都还有不确定性。但恰恰在这里，期权的思路变得非常关键。期权给我们的是一种权利，而不是义务。如果这些业务成功了，上行空间会非常可观；如果不成功，投资者也可以选择离场。所以，不确定性本身反而能带来价值，这也是为什么早一步进场往往胜过苦等尘埃落定。我们的意思并不是说这些未来业务一定全都会成功，而是说 Tesla 的股价同时反映了它现有的业务，以及这些未来期权的潜在价值。

Slide 13 — Close — three points, and how to look at Tesla

Speaker: Hu Kai | Time: 18:30–20:00 | Duration: 90 seconds | English: about 92 words

To close, our three points again. One: diversification, scale, and options. Energy, services and software are a second engine. Cost leadership and automation defend share. And there is option value beyond today's cars. Two: risks are understood, not hidden. Governance and market risk are real. But they are disclosed, monitorable, and priced into the decision. 最后，我们再把三个要点收个尾。第一：多元化、规模效应与期权。能源、服务和软件构成了第二增长引擎；成本领先和自动化守住了市场份额；此外还有超越现有汽车业务的期权价值。第二：风险是看得清、不遮掩的。公司治理风险和市场风险确实存在，但它们都是公开披露、可以持续跟踪，并且已经计入决策考量的。第三：该怎么看 Tesla。不妨跳出下个季度的交付数字，去思考现在的股价是不是合理反映了自动驾驶、机器人和储能背后的长期期权价值。谢谢大家，现在欢迎各位提问。

Appendix — Q&A crib sheet (10 minutes, all four presenters)

Short answers to prepare, in the same pitch framing. English answer first, Chinese translation after. Do not read these aloud unless the question is asked.

Q1. Does Musk's governance problem overturn the investment thesis?

A: No. The agency costs and key person risk are real. Pledged shares, the pay award case, and his attention split across other ventures. But those exposures are disclosed, so you can track them. The board has independent directors. And the courts and the voting rules have visibly pushed back. So we treat governance as a risk to size and watch. Not as a veto on a business and an options case that are both strong.

中文翻译

问：马斯克的治理问题会不会推翻投资论点？答：不会。代理成本和关键人物风险确实存在。股票质押、薪酬方案诉讼，还有精力分散在别的公司。但这些敞口是公开披露的，你可以跟踪。董事会里有独立董事。法院和投票规则也明显往回顶了。所以我们把治理当成需要控制仓位、持续盯着的风险。而不是一票否决——毕竟业务和期权这两块逻辑都很强。

Q2. Is the valuation simply too expensive on a car-only view?

A: On a car-only view it always looks expensive. But that's the wrong test. The gap between the car business alone and the market price is the options question. It's not a reason to walk away. What you're paying for is robotaxis, Full Self-Driving, Optimus, energy and software. Whether that's fair depends on their odds of success, their timing and their volatility. And with option-like payoffs, getting in early can matter more than waiting for certainty.

中文翻译

问：只按汽车业务看，估值是不是太贵了？答：只按卖车算，它总是显得贵。但这个标准本身就用错了。纯汽车业务的价值和市场价格之间的差，正是期权的问题。这不是放弃的理由。你付的价钱买的是 robotaxis、Full Self-Driving、Optimus、能源和软件。这笔钱值不值，取决于它们成功的概率、落地时间和波动率。而且在期权式的收益结构里，进场早往往比等到一切确定更重要。

Q3. How should we use beta when it is only an estimate?

A: Use it for what it measures, and nothing more. Beta measures market sensitivity. So as an ordinary investor, it tells you roughly how hard your holding will move with the market, and what return that sensitivity demands. What it doesn't measure is firm specific risk. And that's the part you can diversify away. Used that way, beta is a handy guide to pricing risk. Not a precise fact. And not, on its own, a reason to reject the stock.

中文翻译

问：贝塔只是一个估计值，该怎么用？答：它衡量什么，就用它看什么，别多用力。贝塔衡量的是市场敏感度。所以作为普通投资者，它告诉你手里的持仓大概会跟着大盘波动多大，以及这份敏感度要求多高的回报。它衡量不了的是公司特有风险。而这部分，分散投资就能化解。这么用的话，贝塔是个好用的风险定价参考。它不是精确的事实。单靠它，也不足以否定一只股票。

Q4. Will competition from BYD and the price war keep hurting the car business?

A: That pressure is real, and we don't hide it. Our point is this. Cost leadership, automation and scale let Tesla live with lower prices for longer than rivals. That protects volume and keeps the cash coming. And energy, services and software keep adding a growing offset. So competition is a reason the moat matters. Not a reason the investment case fails.

中文翻译

问：比亚迪的竞争和价格战会不会继续伤到汽车业务？答：这份压力是真的，我们不藏。我们的意思是：成本领先、自动化和规模，让 Tesla 能比对手更长时间扛住低价。这样就保住了销量，也保住了现金。同时能源、服务和软件还在不断加厚对冲。所以竞争激烈，恰恰说明护城河重要。而不是投资逻辑不成立的理由。
